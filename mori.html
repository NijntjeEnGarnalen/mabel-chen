Mori: MORI is a lifestyle brand that brings the quiet rhythm of growth into everyday life. Centered around a DIY terrarium kit, it invites users to slow down and reconnect through a simple, living system. Designed for urban lifestyles, MORI offers a gentle transition between work and rest, creating a calming, low-effort ritual. Through product, experience, and visual language, it reframes wellness as something that unfolds naturally over time.
